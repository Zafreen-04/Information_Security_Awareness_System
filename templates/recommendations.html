The Role of High School Educators as Security Role Models

The research paper strongly emphasizes that high school teachers are primary role models for youth. When teachers practice poor password habits (e.g. sharing passwords or using simplistic phrases) or fall prey to email phishing, it normalizes risky behaviour among students. Specialized educator enablement programs are essential for establishing school-wide cyber resilience.
