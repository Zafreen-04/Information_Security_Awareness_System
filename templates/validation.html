Validity: Pearson Item-Total Correlation

Measures whether each survey item accurately measures the target construct by correlating each item's score against the composite score of all items.

Reliability: Cronbach's Alpha (α)

Assesses the internal consistency and stability of the scale across repeated observations. High alpha indicates items within the construct measure a shared underlying dimension.

Cronbach's Alpha Formula:
α = (k / (k - 1)) * [1 - (Σ σ²_items / σ²_total)]
Where k is the number of scale items, σ²_items is item variance, and σ²_total is composite variance.
